# Demo script: four beats, under four minutes

Presenter opens in state `midstream` signed in as the VP.

**Beat 1, before (20 seconds).** Lanes view. "This is the tracker AJ keeps tonight." Point at the empty status cells for today. Switch to board view. Deal clock: 9 days to IC, 12 to the bid.

**Beat 2, evidence lands (90 seconds).** Open the inbox. Three proposals. Approve the databook (source line quoted). Approve the contracts summary. The third says "legal is basically done," one line, no attachment; the tool says the evidence is thin and offers "Ask for confirmation." Click it. Back to the board: two cards moved, one has a question out. Open Activity: three entries with the user and time. "Nothing moved without AJ. Every move has its evidence."

**Beat 3, the note (40 seconds).** "Draft tonight's note." The working-group email appears, grouped by workstream, from the two approvals. Edit one line. Send. "Fifteen minutes became ninety seconds, and it is the same email."

**Beat 4, readiness (60 seconds).** Readiness view. Memo sections down the side, coverage across. Two sections unsupported; the board says which workstream owes them. Eight seller questions, three overdue. Bid checklist half done. "Nine days out, the team knows exactly what is missing." Point at the loop rail: "This is stage 2 of 5. Close and Portfolio are next."

Presenter menu (Shift+P): jump to `kickoff` to show the sprint being set up from the playbook, or `ic-minus-3`. "Simulate evidence arriving" drops the lender conflict into the inbox for a question from the room.
