# Demo script: four beats, under four minutes

Presenter opens in state `midstream` signed in as the VP. On the login screen the VP is preselected; the button reads "Sign in as Owen". Every button name below is quoted exactly as the app shows it. Rail tabs under Deal Room are "Board", "Inbox", "Tonight's note", "Readiness", "Activity".

**Beat 1, before (20 seconds).** Click "Lanes" in the view toggle. "This is the tracker AJ keeps tonight." Point at the hatched cells in the Tonight column. Click "Board". Deal clock in the header: 9 days to IC, 12 days to bid. Work strip: 3 waiting on you, 2 blocked.

**Beat 2, evidence lands (90 seconds).** Click "Review 3 proposals" in the action bar. Three proposals, each with the source line quoted and highlighted in the evidence on the right. The first is the databook: click "Approve: mark done". The second is the contracts summary: click "Approve: mark done". The third says "legal is basically done," one line, no attachment. The chip reads "Thin", the proposal says "Thin. One line, no attachment. Ask the sender before the tracker moves." The primary button is "Ask for confirmation", not Approve. Click it. Click "Board" in the rail: two cards read "Done", one reads "Question out". Click "Activity" in the rail, then "Decisions" in the filter: three entries with the user and time. "Nothing moved without AJ. Every move has its evidence."

**Beat 3, the note (40 seconds).** Click "Draft tonight's note" in the action bar. The working-group email appears with a "Draft by the agent" chip, grouped by workstream, from the two approvals. Edit one line in the editor. Click "Send to the working group". The chip changes to "Sent" and the action bar reads "Fifteen minutes of retyping became a read and a click. Same email."

**Beat 4, readiness (60 seconds).** Click "Readiness" in the rail. The strip reads 2 sections unsupported, 3 seller questions overdue, 1 of 6 bid items done. Memo sections down the side, the five workstreams across, one mark per supporting lane, coverage in the last column. The two "Unsupported" rows say "owed by IT" and "owed by IT and HR". Eight seller questions, three marked "Overdue". Bid checklist with "Offer letter draft" ticked. "Nine days out, the team knows exactly what is missing." The five "Placeholder" chips: "These five are the sections we have not confirmed with the sponsor yet. The first five are Greenbriar's own Criteria Worksheet sections." Point at the loop rail: "This is stage 2 of 5. Close and Portfolio are next." Clicking "Close" or "Portfolio Room" in the rail shows a one-line card and "Back to the Deal Room".

**Presenter menu (Shift+P, Esc closes).** Under "Jump to a state": "Kickoff", "Midstream", "IC minus 3", and "Reset this state". Kickoff shows the empty tracker and the playbook card; the primary button is "Set up the sprint". IC minus 3 shows the clock at 3 days and readiness with two sections still unsupported and the bid checklist at 3 of 6. Under "Evidence": "Simulate evidence arriving" drops the lender's "commitment letter sent" email into the inbox for a question from the room. The chip reads "Conflict", both claims sit side by side, and the buttons are "Tracker is right, reject", "Sender is right, approve", and the primary "Ask the sender which is right". Under "Agent": "Mock" and "Live".
